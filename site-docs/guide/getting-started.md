# Getting Started

New to EdgeCoder? The **Getting started** guide takes you from zero to taking part. It opens by
itself the first time you sign in to the desktop app, and it is on the web at
[`/portal/welcome`](https://edgecoder.io/portal/welcome). Reopen it any time from Settings →
Getting started.

*How EdgeCoder works, in 79 seconds (captions on).*

1. **Welcome:** use your own machines (free), buy network compute, both, or host for others (by request).
2. **How EdgeCoder works:** you ask, the coordinator picks the best fit, a machine runs it (yours free, a host's paid), the job travels encrypted, and you pay per token in cSats. Hosts get 95 %, the network 5 %.
3. **Set up this computer:** the desktop app bundles the agent and Ollama and suggests a model for your memory and GPU.
4. **Add your other machines:** the desktop app on Mac and Windows; the installer on Linux and GPU servers. Allow Local Network access on a Mac.
5. **Choose models and where they run:** Auto (recommended), Local first, This computer only, My machines only, or a specific model.
6. **Fund your account:** estimate your cost from live prices, then Buy cSats with Lightning. Skip it if you only use your own machines.
7. **Connect your tools:** GitHub, the hosted API, editors on `localhost:4304/v1`, and `edgecoder code` with `/remote`.
8. **Try it:** a first task, with where it ran and what it cost.
9. **Host for the network:** by request, approved by an admin.
10. **Done.**

The full walkthrough, with commands and every choice explained, is
[docs/getting-started.md](https://github.com/codyrs82/Edgecoder/blob/main/docs/getting-started.md).
