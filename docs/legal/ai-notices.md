# AI and disclaimer notices for the UI

Version: 2026-11-16 · Last updated: 16 November 2026

These strings are used in the apps. Keep them in sync with `src/common/legal-notices.ts` and `desktop/src/lib/legal-notices.ts`.

## Chat and model output (shown under the chat composer)

AI can be wrong. Check answers and review code before you run it. Prompts may be processed on machines run by other people: don't send secrets.

## Network jobs (shown where a job or model call is sent to the network)

This runs on a machine operated by another EdgeCoder user, not by EdgeCoder. They can see what you send. Don't include passwords, keys or other secrets.

## Test mode (shown on wallet and earnings pages while settlement runs on test funds)

Test mode: balances and payouts use Bitcoin test funds and have no real value. They may be reset.

## Payments (shown before paying an invoice or setting a payout destination)

Bitcoin and Lightning payments can't be reversed. Check the invoice or address before you pay or save it; network fees may apply.

## Hosting (shown on the Install agent page and in the installer)

Hosting runs other people's AI and code jobs on this machine. The sandbox reduces risk but doesn't guarantee isolation. Make sure your ISP and employer allow it, and you're responsible for taxes on what you earn.
