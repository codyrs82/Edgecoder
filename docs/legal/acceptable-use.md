# Acceptable Use Policy

Version: 2026-11-16 · Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") is part of the EdgeCoder [Terms of Service](/terms). It applies to everyone who uses the Service: Buyers, Hosts, API users and AI agents acting for someone. "You" includes anyone using your account, keys or agents.

The network runs your work on other people's machines. Misuse puts Hosts, other users and the network at risk, so we enforce this policy.

## 1. Illegal activity

Do not use the Service to break the law or to help anyone else break it, including laws on fraud, intellectual property, privacy and data protection, consumer protection, computer misuse, gambling, securities, money transmission and export control.

## 2. Harmful content

Do not use the Service to create, store, process or distribute:

- child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any content that sexualizes minors. We report apparent CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children and to law enforcement, as required by law;
- content that threatens, harasses, stalks, bullies or intimidates anyone, or that promotes violence against people or groups;
- non-consensual intimate imagery, or content that exploits people;
- content that infringes anyone's intellectual property or privacy rights;
- content designed to deceive in harmful ways, such as fraudulent documents, impersonation, phishing kits or scam material;
- instructions or material meant to enable weapons capable of mass casualties (biological, chemical, nuclear or radiological).

## 3. Malware and attacks

Do not:

- create, test, deploy or distribute malware, ransomware, spyware, botnets, credential stealers or other malicious code for use against systems you do not own or are not authorized to test (see section 7 for authorized security research);
- scan, probe, attack, exploit or gain unauthorized access to any system, network, account or data;
- run denial-of-service attacks or traffic floods, or use the Service as part of one;
- crack passwords, keys or hashes that you are not authorized to recover.

## 4. Attacks on the network, Hosts and other users

Do not:

- try to escape, weaken or test the sandbox on a Host's machine, or run code designed to read, change or exfiltrate anything on a Host's machine or network beyond the job itself;
- abuse Hosts, for example by running jobs designed to damage hardware, exhaust disk, memory or bandwidth beyond the job's stated limits, mine cryptocurrency, or use the Host's IP address for outbound traffic;
- as a Host, read, copy, keep, tamper with or disclose other users' jobs, prompts, data or outputs except as needed to run them; return false, fabricated or copied results; misreport your hardware, benchmarks or work; or run modified agents to get paid for work you did not do;
- interfere with the coordinators, the ledger, the market or payment systems, including replaying or forging signed messages, manipulating prices or witness checks, or colluding to fake verifications;
- probe, scan or test the vulnerability of EdgeCoder systems except under section 7.

## 5. Spam and abuse of the Service

Do not:

- send spam or bulk unsolicited messages, or generate content for spam, SEO manipulation or fake reviews;
- create accounts in bulk or with false information, share or sell accounts, invite codes or API keys, or get around invite caps, rate limits, quotas, spending limits, suspensions or blacklists;
- scrape the Service other than through its published APIs within their limits;
- resell the Service in a way that hides the end user from us when we need to know who they are to enforce this policy.

## 6. Sanctions and export control

Do not use the Service if you are, or are acting for, a person or entity on a U.S. or other applicable sanctions or restricted-party list, or if you are located in a comprehensively sanctioned country or region. Do not use the Service to export, re-export or transfer software, technology or services in violation of U.S. export controls (EAR, ITAR) or applicable sanctions (OFAC). Hosts may not offer their machines from sanctioned jurisdictions.

## 7. Authorized security research

Security research is allowed on the network only within these limits:

- **Only against systems you own, or systems you have explicit written authorization to test** from their owner. Authorization must cover the specific systems, techniques and time window you use.
- Running exploit code, scanners or malware samples in a job is allowed only for that authorized purpose, and only against those systems. It must not target Hosts' machines or networks, EdgeCoder systems, or any third party.
- **Verification.** We may ask you to show your authorization (for example a signed engagement letter or scope document) before or after such use, and may require it before allowing certain workloads.
- **Suspension.** We may suspend jobs, accounts or keys that look like attacks, even if you say they are authorized, until we are satisfied. We are not liable for disruption this causes.
- **EdgeCoder itself.** To report a vulnerability in EdgeCoder, follow our security policy (SECURITY.md in the EdgeCoder source repository) and contact security@edgecoder.io. Do not test against production systems, other users' accounts or Hosts' machines without our written permission.

## 8. Your responsibility

You are responsible for your prompts, code and data, for the output you use, and for making sure your use is legal. AI output may be harmful or wrong even when you did not intend it; review it before you use it.

## 9. Enforcement

We may investigate suspected violations and, without notice where reasonable, remove or block content or jobs, limit features, suspend or terminate accounts, revoke API keys and enrolment tokens, blacklist machines, hold or withhold payouts for work that breaches this policy, and report activity to law enforcement. We decide what is a violation in our reasonable judgment.

## 10. Reporting

Report violations or abuse to abuse@edgecoder.io. Include the job, agent or account identifiers you have.
